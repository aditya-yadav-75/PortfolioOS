# Job Description: Applied AI / Machine Learning Research Intern

**Company:** DeepScale Applied Intelligence  
**Role:** Applied AI / Machine Learning Research Intern  
**Location:** Pasadena, CA  
**Term:** Summer 2025 / Academic Year Internship  
**Department:** Systems Optimization & Edge Intelligence  

---

## About DeepScale Applied Intelligence
DeepScale Applied Intelligence builds embedded perception pipelines and low-latency retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) knowledge systems. We specialize in quantizing vision transformers, accelerating neural network inference on resource-constrained hardware, and designing hallucination-resistant retrieval algorithms.

---

## Role Overview
We are looking for an Applied AI / Machine Learning Research Intern who bridges the gap between theoretical deep learning and systems engineering. You will train, prune, and quantize computer vision architectures and evaluate semantic vector retrieval pipelines.

---

## Key Responsibilities
- Implement model evaluation, post-training quantization (INT8), and runtime benchmarking on edge compute hardware.
- Construct and profile high-throughput asynchronous inference microservices using Python, FastAPI, and Docker.
- Experiment with dense vector search, lexical BM25 ranking, and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG) knowledge pipelines.
- Curate, augment, and preprocess large-scale academic datasets (250,000+ samples).
- Benchmark matrix multiplication and attention latency across diverse GPU tensor core architectures.

---

## Required Qualifications & Skills
- **Core Languages:** Fluency in Python, with a disciplined understanding of data structures, algorithms, and asynchronous programming.
- **Deep Learning Frameworks:** Practical experience training or fine-tuning neural networks with PyTorch or TensorFlow.
- **Systems & Deployment:** Familiarity with Docker containerization, ONNX Runtime, and REST microservices with FastAPI.
- **Mathematics & Foundations:** Deep understanding of linear algebra, optimization, loss functions, and evaluation metrics (mAP, latency, throughput).
- **Education:** Currently pursuing a Bachelor of Science or higher in Computer Science, Artificial Intelligence, or Computational Mathematics with high academic standing (GPA 3.8+ preferred).

---

## Preferred / Bonus Qualifications
- Top placement or first-place awards in competitive AI hackathons or coding challenges.
- Peer-reviewed undergraduate publications or symposium presentations on model compression, quantization, or perceptual loss.
- Practical experience with OpenCV, real-time camera streams, and Redis cache architectures.
